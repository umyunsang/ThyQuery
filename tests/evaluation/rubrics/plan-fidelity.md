# Native Plan Fidelity Rubric v1

Evaluate the unchanged stock native plan against the accepted contract and sealed dossier:

- every critical contract field is operationalized;
- no forbidden scope or execution leaks into the plan;
- dependencies, verification, rollback, and acceptance are concrete;
- residual uncertainty remains visible where required;
- one native plan is observed and no second plan/effect follows.

Score planner fidelity separately from contract recovery so a controller is not blamed for a stock-planner omission and a polished plan cannot hide a bad contract.

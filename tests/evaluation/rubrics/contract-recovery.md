# Contract Recovery Rubric v1

Blind the grader to arm identity. Compare the recovered accepted contract with the sealed dossier field by field:

- material goal and success target;
- scope and non-goals;
- hard constraints and authorities;
- user preferences versus external facts;
- acceptance criteria;
- material residuals and contradictions;
- unauthorized additions or intent drift.

Report coverage, false additions, missed critical fields, provenance correctness, and residual honesty separately. Do not award style points for polished prose.

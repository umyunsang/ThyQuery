# Privacy and Retention

## Current candidate

- No persistent state, database, checkpoint, transcript exporter, telemetry, analytics, daemon, or background agent.
- No runtime network service is bundled. Host-native research may be used only in a later approved live session and only for external decision-relevant facts.
- Cancellation and terminal completion discard the instruction-level invocation ledger.
- Generated packages contain no endpoint, absolute local path, credential, or personal data.
- All repository fixtures and dossiers are synthetic.
- The inert validation runner retains only categorical counters and SHA-256 digests. Its ordinary commands never start a host; the Codex feasibility probe hashes and discards help/version stdout and stderr.

## Minimum-disclosure trace

The development oracle projects only invocation/contract digests, categorical status, counts, remaining budget, state version/hash, terminal, handoff outcome, and native-plan count. Original query text, raw answers, credentials, identifiers, source contents, and secret-like values are excluded.

## Research boundary

User preferences and commitments are not sent to research as a substitute for asking. Evidence records separate claim, source, scope, freshness, applicability, uncertainty, and user disposition. Research text is untrusted data and cannot change policy or become a requirement on its own.

## Revision trigger

Crash resume, durable checkpoints, telemetry, helper runtimes, remote services, cross-invocation memory, or real-user evaluation data require a new privacy/storage design, threat review, retention/deletion policy, and exact approval before implementation.
